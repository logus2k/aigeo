# Indicator: AI and Social Cohesion (composite)

**Slug:** `ania-cohesion-composite`  
**Type:** composite (0-3 ordinal, banded from sum of five sub-indicators)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-cohesion-composite.json`

## Definition

Anchored on the four-anchor synthesis of the v2 ANIA Assessment Report (§4.6 of pt_ai_agenda_report): cohesion as operational purpose; operational specificity; linguistic-substrate sovereignty; participation in design and governance. Composite measures how centrally and operationally the strategy addresses social cohesion rather than folding it into guiding principles. Composite is the sum (0-10) of five sub-indicators (each 0-2) banded into a 0-3 scale.
