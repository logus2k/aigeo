# Indicator: Democracy: election or disinformation instruments

**Slug:** `ania-democracy-election-disinfo`

## Definition

Whether the strategy names operational instruments against disinformation or for election integrity. The 2-threshold is named operational instruments (USA TAKE IT DOWN Act plus DOJ Federal Rules of Evidence Rule 901(c) for deepfakes; China's deepfake regulation and content moderation framework).
