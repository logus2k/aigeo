# Indicator: Ethics-HD: ethics body operational

**Slug:** `ania-ethics-hd-ethics-body`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-ethics-hd-ethics-body.json`

## Definition

Whether the country has an operational AI ethics body with civil-society participation. The 2-threshold is operational with civil-society participation (UK AISI; Spain AESIA; Germany Plattform Lernende Systeme). Overlaps with the Democracy aspect.
