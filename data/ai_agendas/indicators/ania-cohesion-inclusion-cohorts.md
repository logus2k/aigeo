# Indicator: Cohesion: digital inclusion cohorts

**Slug:** `ania-cohesion-inclusion-cohorts`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-cohesion-inclusion-cohorts.json`

## Definition

Whether the strategy commits to numeric cohort-coverage targets including marginal populations. The 2-threshold is a numeric target including a marginal-population KPI (Egypt's 26% marginal-population KPI plus 36% public-access KPI is the only marginal-population quantification in the corpus).

## Score ladder

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | No cohort-coverage target. |
| 1 | Qualitative coverage commitment. |
| 2 | Numeric cohort targets including marginal-population KPI. |
