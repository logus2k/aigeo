# Indicator: Jobs: distributional monitoring

**Slug:** `ania-jobs-distributional-monitoring`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-jobs-distributional-monitoring.json`

## Definition

Whether the strategy commits to monitoring AI's labour-market effects across gender, age, disability, or migrant origin. The 2-threshold is an operational monitoring system or a binding distributional target (for example Brazil's 50% women-researcher binding target, or India's Jan Dhan gender mainstreaming). Overlaps with the Cohesion aspect.
