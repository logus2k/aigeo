# Indicator: Jobs: union or social dialogue

**Slug:** `ania-jobs-union-dialogue`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-jobs-union-dialogue.json`

## Definition

Whether organised labour or social partners are present in the governance of AI's workforce effects. The 2-threshold is institutional representation (for example France's social-dialogue mandate inside the Humanism pillar, or Finland's trade-union representation in the strategic-level roundtable).
