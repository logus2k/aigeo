## Definition

Whether there is a multi-agency oversight mechanism with a stated cadence rather than a single regulator. The 2-threshold is an operational committee with cadence (Australia's AI Review Committee meeting every six weeks, aggregating Information Commissioner, Privacy Commissioner, Ombudsman, and rotating sectoral regulators).
