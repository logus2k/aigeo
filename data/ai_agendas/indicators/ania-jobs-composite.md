# Indicator: AI and Jobs (composite)

**Slug:** `ania-jobs-composite`  
**Type:** composite (0-3 ordinal, banded from sum of five sub-indicators)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-jobs-composite.json`

## Definition

Measures how a national AI strategy treats AI's labour-market consequences: who gets trained, who gets represented in governance of the workforce track, and whether distributional effects across gender, age, disability, and migrant origin are tracked. Composite is the sum (0-10) of five sub-indicators (each 0-2) banded into a 0-3 scale per rubric §7.

## Score ladder

| Score | Verbal anchor | Sum of five sub-indicators (each 0-2) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | absent or principle-level | 0 to 2 |
| 1 | limited operational presence | 3 to 4 |
| 2 | strong operational presence | 5 to 7 |
| 3 | central operational concern | 8 to 10 |

## Sub-indicators aggregated

This composite sums the following five sub-indicators (each 0-2):

- **Workforce training cohorts** (`ania-jobs-workforce-training`)
- **Specialist pipeline** (`ania-jobs-specialist-pipeline`)
- **Union or social dialogue** (`ania-jobs-union-dialogue`)
- **Distributional monitoring** (`ania-jobs-distributional-monitoring`)
- **Productivity target quantified** (`ania-jobs-productivity-target`)
