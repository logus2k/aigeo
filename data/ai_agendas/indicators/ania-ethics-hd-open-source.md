# Indicator: Ethics-HD: open-source commitment

**Slug:** `ania-ethics-hd-open-source`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-ethics-hd-open-source.json`

## Definition

Whether the strategy commits to open-source or open-weight AI as a named priority. The 2-threshold is a mandated open-source or open-weight commitment in named initiatives (USA Action Plan p.4-5 frames open weights as a national bet).

| 2 | Mandated open-source or open-weight commitment. |
