# Indicator: Jobs: productivity target quantified

**Slug:** `ania-jobs-productivity-target`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-jobs-productivity-target.json`

## Definition

Whether the strategy publishes a quantified productivity or GDP-add figure for the AI track. The 2-threshold is a named numeric figure (for example Australia's A\$116 B GDP add and 4.3 pp productivity by 2030, or Portugal's €18-22 B/yr GDP add).

| 1 | Qualitative productivity ambition. |
| 2 | Quantified figure. |
