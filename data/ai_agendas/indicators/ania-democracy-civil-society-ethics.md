# Indicator: Democracy: civil society in ethics body

**Slug:** `ania-democracy-civil-society-ethics`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-democracy-civil-society-ethics.json`

## Definition

Whether civil-society organisations have mandated seats in the country's AI ethics body. The 2-threshold is mandated representation in an operational body. Overlaps with the Ethics and Human Development aspect.

| Score | Meaning |

| 1 | Mention without seats. |
