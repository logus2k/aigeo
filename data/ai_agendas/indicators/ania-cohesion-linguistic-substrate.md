# Indicator: Cohesion: linguistic substrate

**Slug:** `ania-cohesion-linguistic-substrate`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-cohesion-linguistic-substrate.json`

## Definition

Whether the strategy commits to a sovereign-language model paired with a sectoral data-substrate initiative. The 2-threshold is the paired commitment (Egypt's Arabic foundation models paired with Initiative 7 Sectoral Arabic Data Sets; Brazil's PT-BR sovereign model paired with Sovereign Cloud Action 27). Overlaps with Ethics and Human Development sovereign-LLM.

## Score ladder

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | No sovereign-language model commitment. |
| 1 | Sovereign model named without paired data substrate. |
| 2 | Sovereign model paired with sectoral data-sets initiative. |
