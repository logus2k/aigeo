# Indicator: Ethics-HD: AI Act or equivalent

**Slug:** `ania-ethics-hd-ai-act`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-ethics-hd-ai-act.json`

## Definition

Whether the country anchors on a dedicated AI statute or equivalent regulatory regime. The 2-threshold is EU AI Act anchoring or a dedicated AI statute (Japan Act No. 53/2025 is the only standalone AI Act in the corpus; France, Finland, Portugal, Spain, Italy, Germany, Sweden anchor on EU AI Act). China's cluster of sector-based statutes (Generative AI Interim Measures, algorithmic recommendation regulation, deepfake regulation, October 2025 Cybersecurity Law amendment) is read as functionally equivalent in scope.
