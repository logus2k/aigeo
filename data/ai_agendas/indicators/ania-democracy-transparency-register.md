# Indicator: Democracy: transparency register

**Slug:** `ania-democracy-transparency-register`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-democracy-transparency-register.json`

## Definition

Whether the strategy commits to an operational register or national centre documenting public-sector AI use. The 2-threshold is an operational register (Sweden's ATR, Brazil's planned Action 51 National Centre for Algorithmic Transparency, UK's mandatory ATRS for in-scope public bodies).

## Score ladder

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | No transparency commitment for public-sector AI. |
| 1 | Transparency principle stated. |
| 2 | Operational register or planned national centre. |
