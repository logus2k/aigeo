# Indicator: AI, Ethics and Human Development (composite)

**Slug:** `ania-ethics-hd-composite`  
**Type:** composite (0-3 ordinal, banded from sum of five sub-indicators)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-ethics-hd-composite.json`

## Definition

Measures the strategy's treatment of fundamental rights, regulatory regime, sovereign-LLM capacity, and explicit alignment with human-development frameworks. Composite is the sum (0-10) of five sub-indicators (each 0-2) banded into a 0-3 scale.

This composite sums the following five sub-indicators (each 0-2):
