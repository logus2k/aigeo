# Indicator: Cohesion: territorial distribution

**Slug:** `ania-cohesion-territorial`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-cohesion-territorial.json`

## Definition

Whether AI capacity is intentionally distributed across the national territory rather than concentrated in the capital. The 2-threshold is a distributed-hub network (South Africa's AIISA eleven-hub university network targeted by 2025).

## Score ladder

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Nationally anchored without explicit regional distribution. |
| 1 | Regional distribution announced. |
| 2 | Distributed hub network operational or near-operational. |
