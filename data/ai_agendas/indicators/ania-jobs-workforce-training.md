# Indicator: Jobs: workforce training cohorts

**Slug:** `ania-jobs-workforce-training`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-jobs-workforce-training.json`

## Definition

Whether the strategy commits to a quantified workforce-training cohort, signalling that AI literacy is being scaled to a named population rather than left to discretion. The 2-threshold is a numeric target (for example India's IndiaAI FutureSkills 860,000 enrolled, or South Africa's 30% whole-population workforce literacy).

## Score ladder

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | No workforce training target. |
| 1 | Cohort named without a numeric size. |
| 2 | Numeric cohort target. |
