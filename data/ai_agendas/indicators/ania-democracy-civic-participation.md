# Indicator: Democracy: civic participation

**Slug:** `ania-democracy-civic-participation`  
**Type:** sub-indicator (0-2 ordinal)  
**Data file:** `ai_agendas/ania-democracy-civic-participation.json`

## Definition

Whether civic actors participated in the design of the strategy itself, not just consultation on outputs. The 2-threshold is institutionalised participation (Brazil's 117-institution governance for the PBIA design, France's AI Cafés as citizen-deliberation spaces, a participatory drafting methodology).
